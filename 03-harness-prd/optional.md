Juno can automatically read authorized sources, analyze feedback, identify risks, prioritize tasks, check duplicates, and draft recommendations or Jira tickets because these actions have low blast radius and are easily reversible.

Actions such as creating or modifying Jira tickets, updating PRDs, and changing product priorities require PM confirmation because they can affect engineering workflows, product requirements, and delivery commitments.

External communications, contractual decisions, regulatory approvals, and independent strategic commitments are blocked because their impact is high and they may be difficult to reverse.

**The action I would change from Auto to Confirm is Jira ticket creation.** Juno's original instructions allow it to create tickets independently, but incorrect tickets can disrupt engineering workflows and create unnecessary work. Juno should prepare and verify tickets automatically, then request PM approval before creating them.
