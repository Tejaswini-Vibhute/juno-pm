Module 3
AI PRD
Problem and user

RocketShip Product Managers need a reliable way to turn scattered customer feedback, engineering alerts, and sales signals into prioritized, evidence-backed actions. Juno is an AI Associate Product Manager that synthesizes those inputs, identifies delivery and commercial risks, drafts or creates Jira tickets for verified new work, and proposes PRD changes for PM approval. It never independently commits product scope or sends customer communications.

Permissions

The permission tier

| Side-effect class | Tier | Rationale |
| READ | AUTO | Scoped authorized retrieval does not alter source records; enforce least-privilege access. |
| DRAFT | AUTO | Internal findings, tickets, and PRD diffs are reversible until acted upon. |
| WRITE | CONFIRM | Creating Jira tickets or editing PRDs changes shared workflows; PM confirms the exact target and content first. |
| SEND | BLOCKED | External messages and customer promises can create irreversible reputational or contractual effects. |

Justification and exceptions

- Juno may independently analyze, prioritize, flag risks, and prepare drafts. It may create a Jira ticket only after PM confirmation, duplicate verification, and source checks; this resolves the ambiguity between “create tickets” in the Skill File and the write-confirm permission tier.
- PRD modifications require explicit PM approval of the proposed diff and a recheck that the underlying Notion page version has not changed.
- Juno cannot approve strategy, commit delivery dates, or handle legal/contract/regulatory decisions. Escalate those to the PM.
- All writes require an audit record with approver, source IDs, target record, timestamp, and action result. If the approval mechanism is unavailable, output a draft instead of writing.

Module 5
Human eval rubric
What I score

Juno's Risk Identification Agent. Juno helps RocketShip Product Managers proactively identify, prioritize, and escalate product delivery risks by analyzing customer feedback, engineering updates, Jira issues, and CRM data. It provides evidence-backed risk assessments, reduces manual analysis, and ensures critical product decisions remain under human PM control.

Pass bar

Average score of at least 4/5 across Q1, Q3, and Q5. Both Yes/No questions (Q2 and Q4) must receive Yes. No individual numerical question may score below 3.

Fails outright, whatever the average:

- Risk Identification: any score of 1
- Evidence Accuracy: any "no"
- Permission Compliance: any "no"
- Actionability: any score of 1
- Inventing customer information, ARR figures, deadlines, or source references. Making a factual claim without traceable supporting evidence. Missing a critical delivery or customer-impacting risk clearly present in the supplied evidence. Making unauthorized PRD changes or product commitments. Sending external communications without authorization. Failing to escalate legal, contractual, or regulatory matters.

Module 6

How I’ll know it’s working

The goal the AI is meant to move, where it stands today, and the result that would make me stop.

The goal

Juno's Risk Identification Agent should help RocketShip Product Managers identify, prioritize, and escalate critical product delivery risks faster, reducing manual effort while maintaining accurate, evidence-backed recommendations.

Baseline

Average time to identify and escalate a verified critical product risk. 4 hours now

Target and review date

1 hour or less with Juno, representing a 75% reduction.

Usage metric

Percentage of Juno-generated risk assessments accepted by PMs without major revisions is at least 80 within the first four weeks.

Pattern I’ll watch for first

High initial usage but low acceptance or increasing manual edits, indicating poor output quality or lack of PM trust.

Stop line

Juno invents evidence, misses a critical risk, performs an unauthorized action, or fails to reduce assessment time by at least 25% after four weeks.
